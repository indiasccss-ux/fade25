How to Play

Rules and movement mechanics

1 The Goal

Visit every tile on the board exactly once. You cannot re-enter an already visited tile.

2 How Numbers Work (Straight vs Turn)

The starting tile is 1. Keep going the same way to reach 2, then 3. After 3, you must turn; the next tile becomes 1.

Straight: Continuing in the exact same heading (e.g. LEFT → LEFT).

Turn: Changing heading (e.g. DOWN → LEFT) resets count back to 1.

A straight run can contain at most 3 tiles, counting the starting tile. After 3, you must turn.

3 Numbers Fade Away

When your number touches an identical visible number on an adjacent tile, both numbers dissolve.

4 Memory Trap

Faded tiles appear empty, but they are permanently occupied. Stepping on a faded tile ends the game. Tiles that still show a number simply won’t let you in.
